## selected work

### [secThing](https://github.com/Raeid-U/secThing) — local, evidence-first SEC research

A self-hosted research workbench that preserves primary filings, supports full-text, semantic, and hybrid retrieval, and grounds local-AI answers in citation-linked evidence.

`Next.js` `Fastify` `PostgreSQL` `pgvector` `Ollama`

### [RedactThis](https://github.com/Raeid-U/RedactThis) — private redaction before text leaves your machine

A local safety layer that combines deterministic detectors, a private language model, and an independent verification pass to replace sensitive data with stable typed placeholders.

`Go` `Docker` `llama.cpp`

### [SkyForger](https://github.com/Raeid-U/SkyForger) — live market intelligence and forge planning

A market and planning application built around live pricing, player progression, inventory, recursive crafting dependencies, and more than 150 maintained recipes.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Zod` `GitHub Actions`

### [bookThing](https://github.com/Raeid-U/bookThing) — high-throughput archival capture

A responsive Rust desktop application for camera-based archival work, with dedicated preview and capture workers, page management, and portable ZIP exports.

`Rust` `egui` `gPhoto2`

Explore the rest of my work at **[raeid.ca/projects](https://www.raeid.ca/#projects)**.
